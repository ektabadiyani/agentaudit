# Resetting your password

Click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and enter your email address. We send a reset link that expires after **30 minutes** and can be used once.

If your workspace uses SAML single sign-on, you cannot reset your password in Tasktide. Reset it with your company's identity provider (for example Okta or Microsoft Entra ID), or ask your IT team.

If the reset email does not arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you are using the email address on your Tasktide account.
