# Uptime, outages and service credits

Check current service status at **status.tasktide.example**, where you can also subscribe to incident updates.

The **Business** plan includes a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime falls below 99.9% in a month, Business customers can request a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. Requests must be made to support within **30 days** of the end of the affected month. Credits are applied to a future invoice and are not paid as cash.

Free and Team plans do not include an SLA or service credits.
