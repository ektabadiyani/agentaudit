# Refunds and cancellation

You can cancel at any time from **Settings > Billing > Cancel plan**. Only workspace owners can cancel.

- **Monthly plans**: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. We do not refund partial months.
- **Annual plans**: if you cancel within 14 days of the original purchase or renewal date, you receive a full refund. After 14 days, annual plans are not refundable; your plan stays active until the end of the paid year.

When a paid plan ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan. Projects beyond the Free limit become read-only; nothing is deleted.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
