# Transferring workspace ownership

A workspace has exactly one owner. The current owner can transfer ownership to any existing admin from **Settings > Members > Transfer ownership**. The new owner must accept the transfer from the email we send them.

If the owner has left your company and cannot transfer ownership, an admin can contact support. Support will ask for written confirmation from someone at your company with authority over the account, such as a manager on company letterhead, and the process takes 3–5 business days.
