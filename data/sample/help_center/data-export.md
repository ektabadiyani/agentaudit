# Exporting your data

**Project export (all plans):** open a project, click **⋯ > Export**, and choose CSV. The CSV includes tasks, assignees, due dates and statuses. Comments and attachments are not included.

**Full workspace export (Business plan only):** workspace owners can go to **Settings > Data > Export workspace**. We email a download link to a JSON archive with all projects, tasks, comments and attachments. The link expires after **24 hours**.

Exports are limited to one full workspace export per 24 hours.
