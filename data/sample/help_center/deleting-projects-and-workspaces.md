# Deleting projects and workspaces

**Projects:** deleted projects go to **Trash**, where any project admin can restore them for **30 days**. After 30 days they are permanently deleted.

**Workspaces:** only the workspace owner can delete a workspace, from **Settings > Danger zone**. The workspace can be restored by contacting support within **7 days**. After 7 days, deletion is permanent and cannot be undone by anyone, including Tasktide support.

Deleting a workspace does not cancel a paid plan automatically. Cancel the plan first to stop future charges.
