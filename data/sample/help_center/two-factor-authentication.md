# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

You can turn on 2FA in **Profile > Security** using an authenticator app. When you enable it, save the 10 backup codes shown; each code works once.

**Lost your device?**

- Sign in with one of your backup codes, then set up 2FA again on your new device.
- If you are a **member** and have no backup codes, ask a workspace owner to reset your 2FA from **Settings > Members**.
- If you are a **workspace owner** and have no backup codes, contact support. For your security, support staff must verify your identity before resetting 2FA. This takes 1–2 business days and cannot be done over chat.
