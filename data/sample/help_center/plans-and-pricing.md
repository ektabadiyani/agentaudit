# Plans and pricing

Tasktide has three plans:

- **Free**: up to 3 members, 5 active projects, 1 GB of file storage.
- **Team**: $12 per member per month, unlimited projects, 100 GB of storage.
- **Business**: $24 per member per month, unlimited projects, 1 TB of storage, SAML single sign-on, full workspace export, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

Paying annually saves 20% on Team and Business. Prices are in US dollars and exclude applicable sales tax or VAT.

Nonprofits and accredited schools can apply for a 50% discount on Team or Business by contacting support with proof of status. The discount cannot be combined with the annual discount.
