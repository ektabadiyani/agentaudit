# Adding and removing seats

Workspace owners and billing admins can change the number of seats in **Settings > Billing > Seats**.

- **Adding seats**: you are charged immediately for the remainder of the current billing period, pro-rated by day.
- **Removing seats**: the unused time is credited to your next invoice. Credits are not paid out as cash refunds.

Each paid seat allows up to 5 free guests. Guests can view and comment on projects they are invited to but cannot create projects.

Invoices are available in **Settings > Billing > Invoices**. To change the company name or tax ID shown on future invoices, update **Billing details**; past invoices cannot be reissued.
