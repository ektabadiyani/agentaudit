# Integrations

Available on **Team and Business** plans:

- **Slack**: task notifications and creating tasks from messages.
- **Google Drive**: attach Drive files to tasks.
- **GitHub**: link pull requests and commits to tasks.
- **Jira import**: a one-time import of Jira projects into Tasktide. It does not keep the two systems in sync.

The Free plan includes no integrations.

Tasktide does not have a native Salesforce or HubSpot integration. You can connect them through Zapier, which is available on all paid plans.
